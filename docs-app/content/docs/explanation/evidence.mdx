---
title: How to interpret AI-detection evidence
description: Read classifications, fractions, confidence, and segments without invented thresholds.
---

Pangram returns overall and segment-level evidence. Fractions describe the
reported composition; they are not a certainty score. Confidence belongs to
the returned evidence, not to a universal claim about authorship. Keep the
result with other evidence and avoid inventing thresholds the API does not
publish. Pangram CLI preserves Pangram's labels and ordering.
